The problem

Annotation is the bottleneck.
Every robotics team knows it.

01  —  SPEED

Labeling one hour of video takes weeks

200–800 human hours to annotate a single hour of manipulation footage. By the time data comes back, your task requirements have changed.

02  —  COVERAGE

Edge cases stay invisible until deployment

60%+ of robotic perception failures trace back to training data errors. You can't collect rare failure modes in the real world at sufficient volume — or safely.

03  —  TRANSFER

Generic sim data doesn't transfer to hardware

Sim-to-real gaps caused by generic physics assumptions make existing synthetic tools unreliable. You need physics calibrated to your robot, not defaults.

Why it works

Let's address the obvious objection directly.

The concern

"Synthetic data never matches real physics. We've tried it before."

What we do differently

  • We extract physics parameters directly from your footage — inertia, friction coefficients, joint limits, and actuator profiles — not from generic simulation defaults.
  • The physics model is calibrated to your specific robot in your specific environment. Not a generic robot. Not a textbook model.
  • Every generated scenario is validated against the calibrated model before it ships to you. Invalid physics gets discarded automatically.
  • The sim-to-real gap shrinks because the simulation starts from reality. Your footage is the ground truth, not an approximation of it.
